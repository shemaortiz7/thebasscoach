For the player who's stuck on a plateau.

You play in a covers band, a function band, or with friends on a Thursday night. You get through the songs. But the groove doesn't sit the way it does on the record, the harmony goes foggy above the fifth fret, and when someone says "take a solo" you'd rather they hadn't.

More videos won't fix that. Someone watching you play will.

What a lesson looks like.

Every lesson is filmed close on the hands and built as a practice path: small steps you can actually finish.

  • Three angles at once

    The plucking hand, the fretting hand and the whole neck, on screen together.

  • The notes next to the hands

    Tab and counting sit beside the playing, so you never have to guess.

  • Fast, slow, then why

    Each line is played close to full speed, then slowed right down, then explained.

  • A practice path

    The lesson breaks into numbered steps. This one has eleven.

Five strings, five things we train.

The lessons move between these from month to month. All of it is taught in English, on five-string basses. The lines below are strings: press one.

  • Groove and time

    Where the note sits against the drums. Playing behind the beat, on top of it and dead centre, on purpose.

  • Technique

    Fingerstyle, done cleanly. Muting, string crossing, stamina, and a tone that comes from your hands before it reaches the amp.

  • Harmony and the fretboard

    Chord tones, scales and how they join up across the neck, so you know why a line works and can write your own.

  • Improvisation

    Fills, walking lines and solos, built from small ideas you can use at your next gig.

  • Repertoire

    Real basslines from real records, taken apart and put back together.

Questions.

Is this for beginners?

Not yet. It's built for intermediate and advanced players: people who can already get through songs and want to go deeper. If you've just bought your first bass, get a few months of playing in first.

What do I need?

A bass, something to play along to, and a phone. You'll use the phone to watch the lessons, join the WhatsApp group and, on Pro, film yourself for feedback.

How does the video feedback work?

On Pro, you send in a video of yourself playing each month. A coach watches it and sends back a personal video review. Places are capped at 15 so nobody gets a rushed one.

Why Patreon and WhatsApp?

Because you probably use them already. Patreon handles the membership and the payments, so we never see your card details. WhatsApp is where the community lives.

Can I cancel?

Yes. It's monthly, and you can cancel any time from your Patreon account.
