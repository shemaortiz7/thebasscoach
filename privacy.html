Privacy

The short version: this website doesn't collect anything about you.

What this site does

There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no forms on this site. Nothing is stored in your browser. The fonts and scripts are served from this site itself, so your browser doesn't contact anyone else to load the page.

The strings at the top of the home page make sound in your own browser. Nothing is recorded and the microphone is never used.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page, and logs it for security purposes. That is covered by the GitHub privacy statement. We don't have access to those logs.

Patreon and WhatsApp

The membership runs on Patreon and the community runs on WhatsApp. When you join either of them you are using their services, under their own privacy policies. Payments are handled entirely by Patreon; we never see your card details.

Changes

If this site ever starts collecting anything, such as an email address for a waiting list, this page will be updated first to say exactly what, why and for how long.
